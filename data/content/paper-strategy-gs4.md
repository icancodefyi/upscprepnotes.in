# How to Score 100+ in GS4 (Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude)

> Paper: UPSC Mains GS-IV · Strategy Series

## Why this paper matters
GS4 is the most teachable paper: it rewards a clear value system, case study method, and structured expression over volume.

## The realistic target
A 100+ GS4 score is realistic because the syllabus is bounded and the paper rewards method — not encyclopedic knowledge.

## Approach that works
Build a personal 'compass' of values (probity, empathy, integrity) and apply it consistently in case studies with a 4-step method: identify dilemma, stakeholders, principle, resolution + action.

## Common mistakes
Scoring drops sharply when aspirants write philosophy instead of practical, decision-oriented answers, or skip the case-study framework.

## Key takeaway
Internalise one consistent value framework and GS4 becomes your highest-scoring shortcut.
