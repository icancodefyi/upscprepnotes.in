# How to Ace GS2 (Polity, Governance, International Relations)

> Paper: UPSC Mains GS-II · Strategy Series

## Why this paper matters
GS-II carries 250 marks and rewards answer structure over rote facts. It is the paper where disciplined framing lifts an average knowledge base into an above-average score.

## The realistic target
A 120+ GS2 score is achievable for a candidate who has read Polity once deeply and practices 'structure-first' answer writing once a week.

## Approach that works
Open with a direct statement of the issue, define the constitutional/statutory anchor, analyse with 2-3 dimensions, and end with a forward-looking stance. Diagram practice (commissions, flowcharts) earns quick marks.

## Common mistakes
The biggest score leaver is answers that list facts without analysis. UPSC marks the 'so-what': conclusion and value addition, not the number of data points.

## Key takeaway
Practise the structure-first method for 8 weeks and you convert GS2 from a knowledge scramble into a marks bank.
