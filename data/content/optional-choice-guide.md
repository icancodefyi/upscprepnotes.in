# Choosing Your Optional Subject: The Complete Guide

> Paper: UPSC Mains Optional · Strategy Series

## Why this paper matters
The optional is 500 of 2025 mains marks for most, so it can materially move your rank — but the right choice depends on you, not just average scores.

## The realistic target
A well-chosen optional (scored strongly by YOU) is worth more than a 'high-scoring' one you cannot sustain.

## Approach that works
Choose on three tests: (a) syllabus fit with your background, (b) availability of quality material, (c) genuine sustained interest over 6-8 months. Past topper data is one input, not the verdict.

## Common mistakes
The common mistake is chasing last year's 'scoring' optional while ignoring your own bandwidth and material access.

## Key takeaway
Weigh the three tests, not just averages — the subject you can write well covers the gap every time.
