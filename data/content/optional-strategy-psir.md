# Political Science & International Relations (PSIR) — Strategy and Scoring

> Paper: UPSC Mains Optional · Strategy Series

## Why this paper matters
PSIR is a popular optional with a screamable syllabus if you already read Polity and International Relations; its 500 marks can be highly productive.

## The realistic target
A 300+ combined optional score is realistic for candidates with a political-science background who practice answer writing.

## Approach that works
Blend static theory (political thinkers, comparative politics) with live IR current affairs; answer the 'comparative' questions with structured pro-con frameworks.

## Common mistakes
The trap is memorising thinker taglines without being able to apply them to a current event — application is where marks are won.

## Key takeaway
Marry theory to daily IR current affairs and PSIR converts your reading into rank-moving marks.
