# How to Score 120+ in GS3 (Economy, Agriculture, Environment, Security)

> Paper: UPSC Mains GS-III · Strategy Series

## Why this paper matters
GS3 is the most dynamic paper; its questions track current economic and security debates closely. Those who read daily current affairs with a framework score disproportionately.

## The realistic target
A 120+ GS3 score needs concept-level clarity (not news-level) on inflation, banking, agriculture reforms, and climate finance.

## Approach that works
Build a mental 'issue-to-data' map for each major theme, then deploy it as intro + 3 dimensions + data + way forward. Static-news synthesis is the differentiator.

## Common mistakes
Candidates lose marks by writing dated statistics or treating every answer as an essay. GS3 rewards specificity: name the scheme, fund, or index.

## Key takeaway
Connect current affairs to static concepts daily and GS3 becomes your highest paper, not your fear.
