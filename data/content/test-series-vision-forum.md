# Vision IAS vs ForumIAS Mains Test Series — Which One Fits You?

> Paper: UPSC Mains Practice · Strategy Series

## Why this paper matters
Aspirants are split between the two dominant test-series providers, each with a distinct style; the right pick depends on your current scoring stage.

## The realistic target
Understanding the difference saves months of misfit practice and wasted answer-evaluation budget.

## Approach that works
Vision tends toward predictable, syllabus-aligned papers good for stamina and coverage; Forum leans closer to the real paper's unpredictability, better late in preparation. Use one to build, the other to pressure-test.

## Common mistakes
The mistake is treating one series as gospel — no institute matches the real paper exactly; evaluate you, not the provider.

## Key takeaway
Pick by your stage: coverage-first early, unpredictability-first near the exam.
