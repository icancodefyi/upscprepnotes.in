# How to Score 130+ in GS1 (History, Society, Geography)

> Paper: UPSC Mains GS-I · Strategy Series

## Why this paper matters
GS1 rewards breadth and precise factual recall. It is the paper where well-revise-able lists (maps, periods, social issues) convert directly into marks.

## The realistic target
A 130+ GS1 score comes from near-total syllabus coverage mapped to practice; gaps are sharply punished here.

## Approach that works
Anchor each topic to a chronology or a map. For society, tie every issue to constitutionality and government response; for history, link to the modern-national-movement narrative.

## Common mistakes
Overloading with random years and names without context is the classic GS1 trap. Recall must be cued by well-structured revision notes.

## Key takeaway
Master the list-and-map method once and GS1 becomes your most reliable marks.
