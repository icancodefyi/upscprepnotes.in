# GS4 Case Studies: The 4-Step Framework

> Paper: UPSC Mains GS-IV · Strategy Series

## Why this paper matters
Case studies are 50% of GS4 and the most formulaic part of the exam — a reproducible framework converts them from random to reliably scored.

## The realistic target
Mastering the framework pushes your GS4 tender into the 100+ band even on tough papers.

## Approach that works
Step 1 Identify the ethical dilemma (values in conflict). Step 2 Map stakeholders. Step 3 Apply your principle. Step 4 Give a decision with concrete action + justifcation.

## Common mistakes
Aspirants lose marks by moralising abstractly, missing stakeholders, or giving a decision with no practical implementation.

## Key takeaway
Practice 2 case studies weekly with the 4-step method and you internalise the pattern before exam day.
