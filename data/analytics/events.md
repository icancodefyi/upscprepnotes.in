# Analytics Event Taxonomy

## Events to capture
- page_view (all)
- click (CTA + internal links)
- scroll_depth (content engagement)
- free_download_lead (email captured from free host)
- file_download (fulfilled download clicked)
- checkout_started / checkout_completed
- ask_question (content assistant)

## Attributes
- path, referrer, device, session, product_slug (commerce events).

## Why
Lets us measure the funnel: page → lead → download → checkout → paid, per product
and per query. Guardrail: attribute correctly so A/B tests stay clean.
