# Backlink & Directory Outreach
## Targets (first batch)
- UPSC resource directories (5-10 listed).
- Educational portal resource pages.
- Quality guest-post / comparison pages.
- Credible review surfaces (Trustpilot/GBP/ProductHunt).

## Why backlinks matter
Beyond SEO, directories + reviews are LLM-citation trust signals. Even a handful
of credible domains materially boosts discoverability.

## Rule
Only pursue placements that link a real, working page; no paid/fake link farms.
