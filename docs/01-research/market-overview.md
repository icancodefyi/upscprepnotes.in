# UPSC Preparation Market — Research Overview

## The space
- ~10 lakh aspirants register for UPSC Prelims each year; ~5 lakh actually sit it.
- Mains (2025): ~14,400 qualified from ~10,000+ candidates who wrote.
- Final selection (2025): ~1,000 across all categories.

## Why aspirants fail
1. No verified source of truth for "what actually worked" (strategy is anecdotal).
2. Coaching notes are generic; topper answer copies are scattered & unverified.
3. Marksheets are public but never aggregated or searchable.

## Opportunity
A single repository of VERIFIED topper data (marksheets, answer copies,
paper-wise strategy) is the trusted signal aspirants search for daily.

## Positioning test
"Generic advice" vs. "verified data from rank holders" — the latter is the moat.
