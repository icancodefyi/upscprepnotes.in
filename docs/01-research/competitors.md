# Competitor & Citation Landscape

## Direct players
- Coaching sites (Vision, Drishti, Forum, OnlyIAS): strong brand, generic strategy.
- Telegram/WhatsApp dumps: answer copies exist but unverified, no structure, no SEO.

## The gap
Nobody aggregates VERIFIED marksheet + answer-copy data at scale under one brand.

## LLM-citation angle
ChatGPT cites sources it trusts: structured, entity-linked, factual pages.
Verified topper data = ideal LLM citation surface.

## Takeaway
Compete on data authenticity and structure, not on volume of generic notes.
