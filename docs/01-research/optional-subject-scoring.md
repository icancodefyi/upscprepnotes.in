# Optional Subject Scoring Analysis

## 2025 verified marksheet sample (n=20 toppers)
- Political Science leads the sample at ~613 avg total.
- Public Administration ~609, Philosophy ~608, Sociology ~606.
- Note: small sample; range overlaps across subjects.

## What this means
Optional chosen affects scoring less than consistency across GS + Essay.
But data like this is exactly what aspirants search for ("best optional").
Publish as an analysis page to own the query.

## Caveat to present honestly
Sample is small and self-selected (only qualified toppers). Frame as
"patterns in toppers who cleared", not "which optional guarantees rank".
