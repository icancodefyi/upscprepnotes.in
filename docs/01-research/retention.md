# Retention via Free Material

## Model
Free downloads (current affairs, test series, notes) capture email leads.
Email nurture converts leads → paid compilations.

## Why this fits
- Aspirants download heavily; the highest-intent users give email for a PDF.
- A nurture sequence (download → value → offer) is the lowest-cost revenue loop.

## Key metric to instrument
Lead → open → click → paid conversion. Gate every free host page on email.
