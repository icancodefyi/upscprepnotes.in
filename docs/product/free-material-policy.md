# Free Material Distribution Policy

## Rules
- Only distribute material we are legitimately licensed / authorised to.
- No scraping or re-uploading paid coaching content without authorisation.
- Where content is public, link to the source rather than hotlinking dumps.
- Paid compilations must add real value (structure, curation, analysis) — not
  just re-bundled free content.

## Why it matters
Beyond legality, trust is the moat. A platform that hosts infringing copies
loses SEO authority and aspirant confidence, and breaks the "verified data"
positioning.
