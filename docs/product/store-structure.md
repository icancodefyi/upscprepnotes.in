# Store & Compilation Structure

## Products
1. Answer Copies Compilation — curated set of verified copies (+ value-add notes).
2. Top 10 Rankers Strategy — aggregated strategy from highest-ranked toppers.
3. Government Schemes Compilation — schemes reference for GS2/GS3.
4. Anthropology Bundle — optional-specific notes + strategy.
5. Complete GS Notes Bundle — full-syllabus notes.
6. Vision IAS Mains Test Series — practice papers.
7. All Strategy Reports — union of paper-wise strategy.

## Principle
Free item = funnel (email). Paid compilation = curated + augmented.
Never sell what is freely and verifiably available without adding structure.
