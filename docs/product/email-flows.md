# Email Capture & Nurture Flows

## Capture points
- Free material download (primary).
- Store pre-purchase (secondary).

## Nurture sequence (download → paid)
1. Immediately: deliver the download + next-value tip.
2. Day 2: related strategy content.
3. Day 5: compilation offer with clear differentiation.
4. Day 12: scarcity/launch reminder (honest, not fake).
5. Day 30: win-back + new topper additions.

## Metrics
Open %, click %, download→paid conversion. Abandoned-cart follow-up at 1hr.
